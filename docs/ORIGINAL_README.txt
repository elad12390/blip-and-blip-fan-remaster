3 - How to play
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Playing Blip & Blop is quite a simple task. Just use the direction keys 
(default keys : arrows) to move your character and to aim your ennemies. 
There are three basic actions : 

- Shoot with current weapon (default key : left control)

- Jump (default key : left alt)

- Special Cow Bomb (default key: space bar). You don't have it at the beggining
  of the game. You have to deserve it!


There are also two advanced moves : 

Acrobatic jump 
--------------

To make an acrobatic jump, press the jump key twice. Acrobatic jumps allow 
you to cross wider distances but you can't shoot while rolling in the sky. 

Platform crossing
-----------------

By quickly pressing the down key twice, you fall from the platform where 
you are standing. 

You can redefine all the control keys. Just go to the control options.

4 - FAQ
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: When I launch the game, my screen just goes crazy and display weird things, 
what can I do ? 

A: First, please download your latest video card AND monitor drivers. If it 
still doesn't work, use the 'Blip & Blop (safe mode)' shortcut created by
the installation program. The game won't be smooth but it should work. 



Q: How can I play with a joystick/joypad ? 

A: Just press the joypad/joystick button/direction (exactly like if you were 
defining a key) when you redefine the key binding. Note that in order to use 
a joystick/joypad under Blip & Blop, the device must be properly configured 
on your system.
